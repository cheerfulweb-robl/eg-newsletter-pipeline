🎁 For New Residents of East Gwillimbury • Official Welcome Package

The Welcome Hamper

A digital welcome to East Gwillimbury: exclusive savings, gift vouchers, and welcoming coupons from verified local businesses for households newly moved here.

🗓️ Program opens Spring 2027 • Join the priority waitlist now

What it is

The Welcome Hamper is a set of digital coupons from East Gwillimbury shops, restaurants and services. It helps new households discover what is close to home. There is no physical package.

Who can apply

  • You moved to any East Gwillimbury community in the last 3 months.
  • You can confirm your address: upload a utility bill or lease with your name and East Gwillimbury address, or ask for a phone call.
  • One application per household.

How coupons work

Each coupon has its own code (like WG7K3M9Q) and a QR code. Show it at the business; they scan or type it in. Each coupon can be used once.

How it works

  1. 1Apply below and choose how we verify your address.
  2. 2Confirm your email from the link we send.
  3. 3Our staff check your address.
  4. 4Approved households join the waitlist.
  5. 5In Spring 2027 we email you your coupons.

Already applied? Check your application or coupons →

Local businesses: to offer a Welcome Hamper coupon, tick “Contact me about the Welcome Hamper program” when you submit an event, dining special or sale.
