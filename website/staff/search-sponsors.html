📖 Beginner’s Guide: How to Set Up Sponsored Search LinksClick to hide/show
  1. Pick a Word & Destination: Type the search word (for example, pizza) and choose where clicking it takes the visitor (their Welcome East Gwillimbury page, their own website, or search results).
  2. Free or Paid: Check the Complimentary box if you are giving the spot away for free. Every click is counted automatically.

⭐ Quick Search Sponsors

Paid keywords in the Home page's "Quick Search / Sponsored" row. Live keywords appear first, with a small Sponsored tag; automatic suggestions fill the remaining spots (up to 99 quick search pills in total). If more than 99 are live at once, the highest priority wins. Times are Toronto time. Every click is counted here and in the Click Report. A visitor clicking the same keyword again within 30 seconds counts once.
