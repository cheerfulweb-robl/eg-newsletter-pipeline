📖 Beginner's Guide: How to Edit Business Listings & Websites
  1. Fix Wrong Addresses or Websites: Search by business name or click Needs Website Review to find businesses missing an external website or pointing to an internal welcomeeg.ca link.
  2. External Websites Only: A business's Website URL must be its own external site (or left blank to display "No website provided" on its /businesses/ page). Never save a welcomeeg.ca URL as a business's website.
  3. Verify Before Saving: Use the Test Link ↗ button inside the editor to confirm the URL opens the correct business website.
📥 Beginners Guide: Importing bulk businesses from csv file
  1. Upload & Preview CSV: Click Import CSV and select your Google Maps / Places CSV file. All rows import as Pending Approval (status: "pending", source: "csv_import") and are never live or synced to the scraper until you approve them.
  2. Duplicates vs. Different Addresses: Exact duplicates (same name and same street address) are unchecked automatically. Businesses with the same or similar name at a different address (e.g., two locations on Woodbine) are kept checked and highlighted in yellow for your manual review—merge side-by-side unit listings if needed, or approve both separately.
  3. Review Each Pending Business: After importing, the page automatically opens Pending Approval. Before clicking Approve, check and update:
    • Custom Business Photo: CSVs have no photos (listings use the default image until you upload one).
    • Public Description / About: Optional short public bio shown on /businesses/....
    • Listing Type: Switch from Business to Venue / Hall / Park for parks, schools, or community halls.
    • Mapped Category: Spot-check auto-mapped Google types (e.g., mislabeled Ranch, Manufacturer, or Point Of Interest entries).
    • Address, Phone & Website: Fix typos, add missing phone numbers or websites, and reject/ignore non-business junk pins.
    • Private Admin Notes: Add any internal outreach, walk-in, or claim notes (visible to admins only).
  4. Approve & Refresh Sitemap: Click Approve (or Save Changes with Approved in Edit) for each verified business, then click Regenerate sitemap.xml when your batch review is complete.
