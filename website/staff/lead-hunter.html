📖 Beginner's Guide: Using Lead Hunter & Batch Sweep Safely
  1. Single Search (Neighbourhood or Sub-Category): Google returns up to 20 results per call. Search a specific street/plaza (e.g., 213 The Queensway S, Holland Landing) or a narrow sub-category (bakery, florist, barber) so independent local shops aren't buried by chains.
  2. Periodic Batch Sweep (Recommended Every 30 Days): Open the ⚡ Batch Sweep tab to scan all your saved plazas/streets and sub-categories in one throttled run (2.5s between calls). Set your Sweep Reminder Interval (14, 30, 60, or 90 days) so the banner at the top reminds you when it's time to check for new businesses.
  3. Duplicates Set Aside Automatically: Any business already in your directory (same name and street address) or repeated across searches is automatically moved into the collapsible Duplicates Set Aside panel—leaving only new leads in your review table.
  4. Two-Step Cost Control: Searches fetch low-cost Pro fields first. Uncheck chains/out-of-area results (or let Batch Sweep uncheck chains for you), then enrich selected leads for phone, website, and hours.
  5. Zero Photo Copyright Risk & Pending Approval Only: Lead Hunter never imports Google photos (listings use /placeholder-500x500.jpg until you or the owner upload a cleared photo), and pushed leads always land in Pending Approval on /staff/businesses.html—never published live automatically.
