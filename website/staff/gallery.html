📖 Beginner’s Guide: How to Manage the Top Photo StripClick to hide/show
  1. Add or Reorder Photos: Upload community photos or business ads to the clothesline strip at the top of the website, and use Earlier / Later to change their order.
  2. Make Ads Stand Out: Check Sponsored Ad Spot (or Complimentary for free ads) to give a photo a 25% larger shimmering frame and click tracking.

🖼️ Home Gallery

The photos hanging on the clothesline at the top of the Home page, in this order, left to right. The row shows as many as fit the visitor's screen (about 20 on a laptop, about 10 on a phone), so put the most important ones first. Hovering a photo shows its text; clicking opens it full screen. Uploads are resized, watermarked with the Welcome East Gwillimbury logo and saved in /gallery/ on this site. Times are Toronto time.
