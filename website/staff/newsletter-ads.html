📖 Beginner’s Guide: How to Place Ads in the NewsletterClick to hide/show
  1. Pick Which Newsletter & Spot: Choose Monday, Thursday, or Both. Pick which listing number the ad should appear after (for example, position 2 puts it after the 2nd event). If two ads share the same spot, use a for the first and b for the second.
  2. Free / Complimentary Ads: Check the Complimentary box if you are giving a business a free ad spot. (Paid ads purchased on the website are added here automatically.)
  3. Test Before Sending: Open Test Newsletter Dispatch to preview how your ads look inside the email before it goes out to readers.
