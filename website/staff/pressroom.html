📖 Beginner’s Guide: How to Write & Publish a Press ReleaseClick to hide/show
  1. Pick the Business: Choose a business from the dropdown (or type its Name, Address, Phone, and Website in Section 2). This creates the official business contact card that helps Google verify the business.
  2. Let AI Write the First Draft (Optional): Type a few bullet notes (or paste a webpage link) in the blue AI box and click Draft Press Release with AI. Read through the draft and edit any words you want.
  3. Add One Photo: Upload one photo from your computer (or paste a photo link) and write a short description and caption.
  4. Preview & Publish: Click Toggle Live Preview to see how it will look. Click Save as Draft to finish later, or Save & Publish Live to put it on the public Pressroom page right away.
